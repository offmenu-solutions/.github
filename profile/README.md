## How we work
We follow a clear, delivery-oriented process. Each phase has an outcome, and we validate the work before moving forward.

1. **Understand** --> map the current process, pain points, tools, and desired outcome.
2. **Design** --> define the solution, architecture, integrations, and expected costs.
3. **Address data & security** --> identify the data involved, responsibilities, providers, and safeguards.
4. **Build & test** --> implement the workflow or software and test it, including failure scenarios.
5. **Deploy** --> validate with the client, configure access, document the solution, and provide handover.
6. **Maintain** --> monitor, handle incidents, manage changes, and keep the solution running.

> We aim to validate solutions end-to-end before putting them into production. Where appropriate, we use synthetic or pseudonymized data, least-privilege access, and human review at the points where it is useful.


## Our principles
- **Tailored by design**. Each client and process is different.
- **Clarity over hype**. We explain what we build and how it works.
- **Practical technology**. Automation first; AI only when it makes sense.
- **Transparency**. The solution should be understandable, not a black box.
- **Security and privacy considered from the start**. Data flows, access, providers, and operational safeguards are part of the design.
- **Human-centered delivery**. Human validation is introduced where the client or process needs it --- without adding unnecessary friction.
- **Visible value**. We want clients to see the impact through useful outcomes and clear reporting.
